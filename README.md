# My Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio website!

## How to Activate GitHub Pages

Follow these steps to make your portfolio accessible online:

1. **Go to Repository Settings**
   - Navigate to your repository: https://github.com/MLCarter1976/my-portfolio
   - Click on the **Settings** tab

2. **Enable GitHub Pages**
   - Scroll down to the **Pages** section (on the left sidebar under "Code and automation")
   - Under "Build and deployment":
     - Select **Source**: Choose "Deploy from a branch"
     - Select **Branch**: Choose `main` and `/root`
     - Click **Save**

3. **Wait for Deployment**
   - GitHub will build and deploy your site automatically
   - You'll see a message confirming your site is published

4. **Access Your Site**
   - Your portfolio will be live at: `https://MLCarter1976.github.io/my-portfolio`
   - It may take a few minutes to appear after you enable Pages
